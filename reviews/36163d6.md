## Correctness checks that passed

- **Bitwise unchanged**: all 1632 rectangular probe grids (jittered or not, F32/F64, 1–3-D, periodic, anisotropic)
  and all unjittered sphere grids match 0a10453. Lattice-aligned boxes never fill `near` (early return), and on an
  unjittered lattice no snapped point comes within h/2 of an interior point, so no new clash can fire. Changing
  the keys (`mins`-relative, wrapped) changes which cell a point is in, not the outcome.
- **Snapped points stay interior and on the wall; ghosts stay ≥ h/2 out**: min ghost φ ≥ 0.500 h in all 192
  `prop.jl` grids and in the spacing probes. Every on-wall interior particle keeps ≥ h/4 (minimum image) from every
  other interior particle, also across periodic seams.
- **Order and scope**: the interior set is hashed before the loop, so snapped points meet both the interior and
  the earlier snapped points. `signed_distance(geom.shape, ·)` is safe because `near` is non-empty only for
  shape-based domains.
- **Generic code**: `T`/`D` generic, no Float64 literals; host-only code (not a kernel), so the Polyester and
  kernel rules do not apply.

## Verdict

The commit fixes D2 and D4 in the code and documents D1 and D3 accurately. On jittered clouds a snapped point now
keeps at least h/4 from every interior particle (worst 0.2501 h over 192 grids, was 0.005 h), also across periodic
seams. Ghosts stay at least h/2 outside. Lattice-aligned and unjittered grids are bitwise unchanged, and the cost
is negligible (≤ 4 ms of `get_points` at 400² / 64³). The suite passes. The only gap is that the new test does not
fail without the fix (E1).

Earlier findings: 4 OK (D2, D4 fixed; D1, D3 documented, D1 still open as a numerics problem), 0 NOT OK.
