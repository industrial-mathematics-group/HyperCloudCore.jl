## Findings

### N1 (medium, CONFIRMED, regression) — a fully covered particle's zero volume turns into NaN in the removal rounds when a piece moves more than about one spacing per sweep
`_update_region!` now sets `V[j] = 0` for a particle whose whole share is covered (`MovingBoundaries.jl:150-155`).
Such a particle is removed in some round, but until then it sits in the local sets of other rounds' events as a
survivor: `_remap_event!` gives it `Vn = max(V + δV, 1e-12 V) = 0` (`Adaptivity.jl:553`; δV = 0 when no
quadrature point of its support is in the region), the residual correction gives it `frac = 0`, and
`Un = mn / Vn = 0/0` (`Adaptivity.jl:575`). The NaN is written back, then enters `m_before` (`0 · NaN`) and the
reconstruction bounds of the next events. `q2_fast.jl` (2-D plane piston advancing, h = 1/40, sweeps only):
\|w\|Δt = 0.5 h and 1 h fine; **1.5 h and 3 h: NaN** (periodic and walled). `q2b_nan.jl`: after the second sweep 20
particles 1 h inside the piece have V = 0, and the sweep leaves NaN at **200 particles up to 10 h from the face**. The
parent never produces NaN (it keeps the volume; errors 0.48/0.72, Σ V off by up to 0.03). The step is above the
documented limit (\|w\|Δt ≤ Δx/4), but the time step estimate does not know the wall speed (a piston faster than
about 3× the signal speed at CFL 0.5 gets there), and a NaN is not a graceful degradation.
- Fix: drop zero-volume particles by compaction before the rounds (they carry no volume and no content, nothing to
  remap), or treat `Vn == 0` survivors as removed in `_remap_event!` (no write, not in `aff`); warn per grid when
  `max |w| Δt > clearance · dx`.

### N2 (medium, CONFIRMED) — the inserted wall rows carry a persistent error; behind a receding piston along walls the uncovered part is less accurate than on the parent
`q9_accuracy.jl`/`q15_uncovered.jl`: 2-D piston receding at the flow speed in `[0,2]×[0,0.5]` (walls y = 0, 0.5,
`MirrorBC(1)` on walls and face), data `1 + ½ cos 2π(x−t−1) cos 2πy` with zero normal derivative on the face and the
walls (so every condition is consistent), error in the uncovered part `1+3h < x < face−3h` at t = 0.5:

| N | fix (max / where) | parent |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 20 | 0.080, wall row | 0.098, rows 3–4 |
| 40 | **0.115**, top wall row | 0.087, row 5 |
| 80 | **0.062**, top wall row | 0.037, row 5 |

The rows off the wall are as good as or better than the parent's (N = 80 rows 1–5: 0.036/0.022/0.016/0.020/0.030
against 0.012/0.010/0.016/0.029/0.037), the initial part is identical (0.0050). The inserted wall-row particles are
the problem (`q16b_top.jl`, `q17_track.jl`, N = 40): they are born with errors of +0.066 (top) / −0.042 (bottom),
the bottom one decays, the top one stays at ≈ +0.07 for the rest of the run, and the top row's error grows towards
the face (0.007 at x = 1.075 … 0.115 at x = 1.4). Their volumes are 0.589 h² against 0.970 h² for wall-row particles
of the generated cloud (row 1: 0.87 vs 0.945): the remap's partition of a half cell at the wall is not the
estimator's. The extra ghost row (N6) is not the cause (`q15b_override.jl`: unchanged with the row restored). The
top/bottom asymmetry for symmetric data suggests an order dependence (rounds and lattice run in index order); not
pinned down. The commit's test checks only that ghosts and wall rows exist.
- Fix: find the cause before relying on the wall rows (compare an inserted wall-row particle's remap share and
  state with the generated one; try a local volume re-estimate for inserted particles); add an accuracy test of
  this case with consistent data (it is cheap at N = 20/40).

### N3 (medium, CONFIRMED, regression) — the builder path makes adaptivity unusable together with moving pieces
`build_adaptivity` sets `volume_estimator = ShepardVolumes()` whenever `_is_moving(pg)` (`src/Builders/
adaptivity.jl:27`), and `_is_moving` is true for a static cloud with moving pieces. `adapt_particles!` now throws for
that combination (`Adaptivity.jl:93-95`). `q13_builder_adapt.jl`: plate channel, `build_adaptivity(Dict(:enabled =>
true), ctx)` → `ShepardVolumes`, `adapt_particles!` → ArgumentError. A config-driven run (HyperCloud) with
`Adapt.enabled` and a moving piece fails at the first adaptivity call, although the combination with
`volume_estimator = nothing` is supported (the remap uses the region at `boundary_time`).
- Fix: in `build_adaptivity` use `!(pg.mover isa NoGridMover)` instead of `_is_moving(pg)`; add a builder test.

### N4 (low, CONFIRMED) — the rounds make sweeps with many events 1.2–1.8× slower; a 3-D row removal costs seconds (parent too)
Each round does a full neighbour search, an O(N) candidate scan (`_covered_particles`) and the slot count; the
number of rounds per row grows like (face extent / event spacing)^(D−1), with an event spacing of 13.1 h (2-D) and
14.6 h (3-D). `q5_cost3d.jl`, plane piston advancing ¼ spacing per sweep (a row every 4 sweeps), 2 threads, sweep
time mean / max (the max is the row-removing sweep) and allocation per sweep:

| case | particles | neighbour search | fix | parent |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 2-D N = 40 | 1760 | 0.17 ms | 7.2 / 52 ms, 3.4 MB | 4.8 / 33 ms, 3.7 MB |
| 2-D N = 80 | 6720 | 0.46 ms | 11.3 / 51 ms, 7.2 MB | 9.2 / 28 ms, 8.4 MB |
| 3-D N = 12 | 2304 | 2.2 ms | 0.45 / 2.3 s, 305 MB | 0.34 / 1.7 s, 328 MB |
| 3-D N = 16 | 5120 | 4.6 ms | 1.1 / 5.7 s, 907 MB | 0.76 / 3.8 s, 981 MB |
| 3-D N = 24 | 16128 | 14 ms | **3.2 / 15.8 s, 3.1 GB** | 2.2 / 10.9 s, 3.3 GB |

Most of the 3-D cost is the per-event remap itself (local sets of radius 12.2 h, a 20³ quadrature box, ≈ 26 MB of
allocation per removed particle: 30.5 GB over 1152 removals), present on the parent (29 MB); the rounds add
1.2–1.8× on top (row-removing sweep: 2-D 52 vs 33 ms and 51 vs 28 ms, 3-D 1.35–1.5×). A 3-D piston at N = 24
spends ≈ 3 s per step in the sweep against a neighbour search of 14 ms: 3-D moving boundaries are not practical
yet. Not mentioned in the docs (their cost line is the 2-D plate).
- Fix: remap each connected cluster of events as one event (all removed and added particles of a face patch in one
  quadrature and balance: one remap per patch instead of one per particle, and no rounds), or update the neighbour
  lists locally after a round; preallocate the remap scratch. State the 3-D cost in the docs.

### N5 (low, CONFIRMED, pre-existing) — wall-row particles next to an advancing piece are removed only once covered
`_covered_particles` removes a particle when its nearest surface is a moving piece and `φ > −clear`
(`MovingBoundaries.jl:308`); for a particle on a static wall the wall (φ = 0) wins the tie until the piece covers it.
`q12_wallrow.jl` (\|w\|Δt = 0.2 h, within the limit): wall-row particles reach 0.000 h from the face, 24 interior
particles are inside the piece before a sweep, and the "moved more than the clearance" warning fires at a legal
step. Same on the parent; more relevant now that H3's fix inserts wall rows behind receding pieces (an oscillating
piston meets them on the way back).
- Fix: in `_covered_particles` test the moving pieces' own signed distances (`signed_distance(at_time(piece, t), x)`),
  not the composite's nearest surface.

### N6 (low, CONFIRMED) — `_sweep_lattice` drops the outermost row at the upper end of a box by round-off
`floor(Int, (hi - canvas_min) / dx)` (`MovingBoundaries.jl:319`) with `hi = canvas_max` gives 25 instead of 26 for
`(0.575 + 0.075) / 0.025 = 25.999999999999996` (`q16c_dbg.jl`). Behind the receding piston the top wall gets 2 ghost
rows instead of 3 (38 ghosts against 55 at the bottom, `q16b_top.jl`); the same applies to the insertion lattice when
a box ends at `canvas_max`. No accuracy effect in N2's case (`q15b_override.jl`).
- Fix: index ranges with a tolerance, or iterate the lattice indices of the canvas (as `get_points` does) and clip by
  index.

### N7 (low, CONFIRMED) — static ghosts keep their t = 0 normals near a piece; new Dirichlet ghosts copy their neighbour
- q4a: static ghosts that were near the piston's initial corner keep the corner normal and distance after the piston
  left: max |Δn| 0.60 and |Δφ| 0.022 (0.44 h) against the regenerated cloud, at (1.1, 0.6). The documented limit
  covers a piece's own ghosts, not the static ones it leaves behind. Fix: re-evaluate `wall_geometry` of the static
  ghosts in the boxes when the sweep adds ghosts.
- `_add_static_ghosts!` gives a new ghost the state of the nearest ghost of its tag (`:413`); for `FixedDirichlet`
  with data varying along the wall the value is carried along as a constant: g = 1 + x on the walls, 26 new ghosts,
  max |U − g| = 0.30 = the distance the piston moved (q4a). Fix: document, or let `FixedDirichlet` take a data
  function for new ghosts.

### N8 (low, CONFIRMED) — the periodic-seam refusal has false positives for rotating pieces
`_check_periodic_pieces` uses `bounding_box(at_time(piece, t))`, the box of the rotated corners of the body's box
(`Shapes.jl:585-595`), which for a rotating ball reaches `r√2` from its centre. `q6_seamcheck.jl`: a ball (r = 0.1)
rotating about its own centre with its edge 0.020 or 0.030 from the seam is refused at t = π/4 (box up to 1.0214 /
1.0114) although it never crosses; at 0.045 it passes.
- Fix: check with the signed distance (sample the shape's surface or evaluate `signed_distance` of the posed piece on
  the seam plane), or use a tighter box for shapes that have one (`Ball`: centre ± r).

### N9 (low, CONFIRMED) — 405f797: `T` is lost in multi-statement strings; parse errors read worse
`ex = :(let T = $T; $(Meta.parse(shape)); end)` (`src/Builders/domains.jl:110`). `q8_string.jl`/`q8b.jl`:
- `"r = T(0.5); Ball((T(0), T(0)), r)"` → `Meta.parse` returns a `:toplevel` expression, whose statements are
  evaluated at the top level of `Main` outside the `let`: **UndefVarError: `T` not defined in `HyperCloudCore`**
  (the fallback's module), for both float types. `"begin …; … end"` works; `"r = 0.5f0; Ball(…, r)"` (no `T`) works.
- A syntax error is now reported as `syntax: invalid syntax (incomplete #<julia: Base.Meta.ParseError(…)>)` instead
  of the `ParseError` itself.
- A global `T` the user defined in `Main` is shadowed (`"Ball((0.0, 0.0), 1.0 * T)"` with `Main.T = 3` worked on the
  parent, fails now); assignments no longer leak into `Main` (an improvement).
- Fix: parse first, rethrow `:incomplete`/`:error` as the ParseError, turn a `:toplevel` into a `:block` before
  wrapping it in the `let`; state the `T` binding in the docstring (done) and the block rule.
The `eltype` check and its message are unchanged and correct (q8: a Float64 string in a Float32 run is refused).

### N10 (low, CONFIRMED) — the catch-up sub-steps are counted from the canvas corners
`_sync_substeps` bounds the displacement of the rigid map at the canvas corners (`TimeSteppers.jl:304-325`), so a
rotation far from the corners needs many sub-steps although the piece barely moves: a ball (r = 0.1) spinning at
ω = 2 in [−1,1]² (h = 1/40) needs 64 / 512 / **4096** sub-steps (each a `place_ghosts!`, neighbour search and sweep)
for t₀ = 0.1 / 1 / 5 (`q7_sync.jl`). Correct, only slow. Fix: use the pieces' (posed) bounding boxes widened by the
band instead of the canvas corners.

### N11 (low, CONFIRMED, pre-existing) — the insertion step fills holes anywhere in a piece's box
`_uncovered_points` takes every lattice point of the boxes that is inside the region and `min_gap` from all
particles, not only what the piece uncovered; a piston's box is the whole canvas. `q14_jitter.jl`: with 45 %
jitter one sweep that moves the face by 0.025 h inserts **87 particles up to 40 h from the face** (parent: 90).
20 % and 35 % jitter: none. Fix: restrict insertion candidates to lattice points the region pass found uncovered
(plus their `min_gap` neighbourhood), or document.

## Verdict

The central fix works: running the sweep's removals and insertions in rounds of independent remaps removes the
far-field corruption (2-D one-row removal 0.2 → 0.008, 3-D range [0.875, 2.125] → [0.9953, 2.0047], co-moving plate
no longer diverging away from the plate), the remaps are exactly conservative, the periodic seam is counted and
inserted once, the swept cloud now reproduces a freshly generated cloud including the static walls' ghosts and wall
rows, and M1–M3, L1, L2 are fixed (M1/M3 by refusal) with tests that fail on the parent. KA parity of the changed
kernel is bitwise, the suite is green, and the docs' numbers reproduce. New problems: zeroed volumes turn into NaN
when a piece moves ≳ 1.5 spacings per sweep (N1, regression), the inserted wall rows carry a persistent error that
makes the uncovered region behind a piston along walls less accurate than before (N2), the builder makes adaptivity
fail with moving pieces (N3, regression). M5 (cost of a moving step) is unchanged and now documented for the 2-D
plate; a 3-D row removal costs seconds (10.9 s on the parent, 15.8 s with the rounds at N = 24, N4).

Earlier findings: H1, H2, M1, M2, M3, L1, L2 fixed; H3, M4, L3 partly fixed; M5 not fixed (documented).
New findings: 3 medium (N1–N3), 8 low (N4–N11); all confirmed (N2's cause suspected, not pinned down).
