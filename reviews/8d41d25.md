## Findings

### N12 (medium, CONFIRMED, from 405f797; the docs misattribute it) — the insertion rounds leave a non-convergent error 3–12 spacings behind a receding face
`r8_n2loc.jl`: the receding co-moving piston along walls of `q9` (consistent data, `MirrorBC(1)` on face and
walls), max error in the uncovered part by distance to the face, t = 0.5:

| N | 8d41d25 [3,6) h | [6,12) h | prev [3,6) h | parent [3,6) h | [6,12) h | 8d41d25 with one insertion round (`r8b`) |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 20 | 0.0643 | – | 0.0802 | 0.0980 | – | – |
| 40 | 0.0449 | 0.0062 | 0.1149 | 0.0870 | 0.0068 | 0.0876 |
| 80 | 0.0586 | 0.0105 | 0.0604 | 0.0534 | 0.0051 | 0.0540 |
| 160 | **0.0635** | **0.0177** | 0.0635 | **0.0133** | 0.0029 | **0.0150** |

The error sits mid-channel (y ≈ 0.3), 3–6 h from the face, positive (`r9_vols`: +0.059 / +0.064 at N = 80 / 160),
and grows with N in the [6,12) h band. The parent converges (≈ 4× from 80 to 160) and so does 8d41d25 when
`_spaced` returns every candidate (one round of events, probe-only override; this receding case removes almost
nothing, `q1` counts 2 removals in its first sweeps, so it is in effect one round of insertions): the rounds for
insertions (405f797) cause it. rr1 attributed the N = 80 excess to the wall rows; with the wall rows gone it remains. The new
docs line "largest a few spacings from the face (the slow convergence at a co-moving face above)" is therefore
wrong: at the same face the parent reaches 0.013. The volumes are not the cause (8d41d25: V/h² 1.000–1.085 in that
band; the parent's are 0.69–1.32 and it still converges).
- Fix: insert all candidates of a sweep in one round (the removals need the rounds, H1; the insertions only use
  them to avoid inserting a periodic seam point twice, which a dedup of the candidates by wrapped lattice index does
  as well), or find the bias of sequential insertion remaps; add an accuracy test at N = 80/160 (the case costs
  ≈ 12 s at N = 160); correct the docs line.

### N13 (medium, CONFIRMED, regression of 8d41d25) — without inserted wall-row particles the corner error with `ExtrapolationBC` grows, wall rows a piece passes are lost for good, and curved walls get wider gaps
The insertion test `signed_distance(shape_t, x) <= -gap` (`MovingBoundaries.jl:387`) excludes every lattice point
within half a spacing of any static wall:
- **Corner error.** `r8c_extrap.jl` (as N12, `ExtrapolationBC(1)` on the face, consistent data): overall max error
  0.121 / 0.193 / 0.191 / **0.319** for N = 20 / 40 / 80 / 160, at the particle one spacing from the face and from
  the bottom wall ((1.4938, 0.0062) at N = 160). Prev 0.148 / 0.136 / 0.088 / 0.104, parent 0.252 / 0.165 / 0.070 /
  0.024. With the insertion condition of 405f797 restored in-process (`r8d_wallrows.jl`, reach kept): 0.080 /
  0.086 / 0.103, the corner error gone; `r8e_norefresh.jl` (no static-ghost refresh) changes nothing. `q9`'s
  ExtrapolationBC line shows it too (0.121 / 0.193 / 0.191 vs prev 0.148 / 0.136 / 0.088). The N2 fix was measured
  only with `MirrorBC` on the face.
- **Lost wall rows.** Wall-row particles that a piece removes (now promptly, N5) are never re-inserted: an
  oscillating piston leaves 208 interior particles at the initial pose after each period against 220 on prev and
  222 at t = 0 (`q4b_osc`); a plane passing through the upper chamber of `r3` removes the 9 wall-row particles at
  y = 1.0, x ∈ [0.8, 1.0] permanently (`r3c_lost`), on a wall no moving piece touches.
- **Curved walls.** Lattice points less than h/2 inside a curved static wall, which `get_points` keeps, are not
  inserted (`r4_curved`, disc r = 0.8 behind a receding face, h = 1/40): none in the uncovered part against 22 in a
  generated cloud and 6 on prev; largest distance from the arc to the nearest particle 1.42 h (prev 1.08 h,
  generated 0.65 h, initial part 0.71 h).
- Fix: address the volume of the inserted wall particle instead of dropping it (rr1 measured 0.59 h² against
  0.97 h²: e.g. re-estimate the volumes of the inserted particle and its neighbours locally, or give it the share
  the generated cloud would have), or at least keep `gap` from moving pieces only and insert static-wall lattice
  points; test the receding piston with `ExtrapolationBC` on the face and an oscillating piston (wall-row count after
  a period).

### N14 (low, CONFIRMED, regression of 8d41d25) — removal by the pieces' own distances also fires where a piece's surface is not a wall
`_covered_particles` removes a particle when `abs(signed_distance(p, x)) < clear` for any posed piece
(`MovingBoundaries.jl:342`), including parts of the piece's surface that lie inside the region (a piece combined
with static parts by union or intersection). The insertion test is the composite's (`:387`), so the same lattice
point is re-inserted in the same sweep. `r3_phantom.jl`/`r3b_phantom_dbg.jl`: fluid = box ∩ (H ∪ upper chamber),
H a translating half-space (0.2 h per sweep), the plane x = face continues through the upper chamber: in each of the
2–3 sweeps in which the plane is within 0.25 h of a lattice column, 19 particles there are removed and 19 inserted
at the same points; after 40 sweeps without a PDE the state there is off by 0.030 (prev 0.0061, advancing and
receding alike), and the top wall row loses 9 particles for good (N13). The old test (nearest surface of the
composite a moving piece) had no such case but lost N5's tie.
- Fix: count a piece only where its surface is the region's boundary, e.g. `abs(signed_distance(p, x)) < clear &&
  abs(signed_distance(shape_t, x)) < clear + tol`, or keep `_probe` and break the tie at φ = 0 in favour of the
  moving piece.

### N15 (low, CONFIRMED, pre-existing) — static-wall ghosts are never removed: an advancing piece runs over the ones a receding phase added
`r10_refresh.jl` (oscillating piston along walls, h = 1/20): at the inner turning point 62 static ghosts (tags 3/4)
lie inside the piston, 50 of them not in a cloud generated at that pose; against the generated cloud their normals
differ by up to 1.17 and their wall distances by 2.2 h. Same on prev (74 / 62, Δn 1.17); 8d41d25's refresh only
reduced the static ghosts with an x-normal (13 → 1). They lie in the stencils of the interior particles at the
corner (R = 2.5 h).
- Fix: drop static ghosts that are inside a moving piece (or farther than `R + dx` from every interior particle)
  after the sweep, as the band of `get_points` would.

## Minor issues

- `sweep_boundaries!` docstring is stale: "lattice points … inside the region, at least `min_gap` spacings from every
  moving piece … (the lattice points of a static wall lie on the wall, as in the initial cloud)" (`:168-170`; now
  `min_gap` inside the region by the composite distance, within reach, no wall points), "A piece whose box reaches
  across a periodic end is refused" (`:188`, and the docs' Limits line; now: a piece with points on the seam), and
  "`mass_residual` … the content of what was covered and uncovered" (`:187`; the docs' new Limits line is right).
- `_sweep_lattice`'s tolerance `T(1e-6)` (`:366`) is in index units, below Float32 round-off: for a Float32 canvas
  `(0.5 + 3dx − (−3dx)) / dx` gives 31.999996 for dx = 1/52 and 4005.9993 for dx = 1/8000, and the last row is still
  dropped (arithmetic checked in Float32; not reproduced on a grid). A relative tolerance (`8 eps(T) · n`) or
  integer lattice indices would hold for both types.
- `_check_periodic_pieces`: a piece that has moved entirely past a periodic end in one sweep (box [1.02, 1.07] for a
  domain ending at 1) passes, because neither end lies in its box (`:305`; `r5`: 8 interior particles inside the
  plate's wrapped image, Σ V 2.0000 for 1.9950). Needs a step far above the clearance; `lo[d] > maxs[d] || hi[d] <
  mins[d]` could be refused outright.
- String shapes: newline-separated statements are still a `ParseError` ("extra token after end of expression");
  `Meta.parseall` would accept a multi-line config string. The incomplete-code message embeds the `repr` of the
  `ParseError` (`"…: Base.Meta.ParseError(\"ParseError:\\n# Error @ none:1:19…"`); `sprint(showerror, …)` reads better.
- The warning count: when `_drop_empty!` removes particles, `n_inside` is set to that number and the particles still
  inside a piece among the remaining candidates are not counted (`:236`).
- Initial cloud (pre-existing): the corner lattice points where a moving face meets a static wall stay interior on
  the face (the static wall wins the tie in the push-out, `Domains.jl:326-330`), so the first sweep warns about
  covered particles at a legal step (`r1_q12dbg`).
- `_refresh_static_ghosts!` runs only when the sweep added ghosts and only in the boxes; the docs say "the static
  ghosts in the region take the wall geometry at the current time" without the condition.

## Verdict

8d41d25 fixes what it set out to fix in the cases rr1 measured: no NaN for fast pieces (and `_drop_empty!` only
removes particles 1–2 spacings inside a piece, conservatively), the builder works with moving pieces, removal no
longer lets wall-row particles reach the face, the canvas-edge row, refreshed static normals, the seam check without
false positives at microsecond cost, multi-statement strings, 16× fewer catch-up sub-steps, insertions only near a
piece; KA parity is bitwise, the suite is green, and every number in the docs reproduces. The N2 fix trades one
error for others, however: the error behind a receding co-moving face does not converge (0.064 at N = 160 against
0.013 for the parent), which is caused by the insertion rounds of 405f797 and not by the co-moving face as the docs
now say (N12); without inserted wall rows the corner error with `ExtrapolationBC` on the face grows with N
(0.32 at N = 160), wall rows a piece passes are lost and curved walls get wider gaps (N13). Removal by the pieces'
own distances churns particles where a piece's surface is not a wall (N14), and static ghosts left inside a returning
piece persist (N15, pre-existing).

Earlier findings (rr1): N1, N3, N5, N7, N8, N9, N10, N11 fixed; N6 fixed for Float64; N2 partly fixed; N4 not fixed
(documented).
New findings: 2 medium (N12, N13), 2 low (N14, N15); all confirmed (N12 is from 405f797, N15 pre-existing).
